# The Power of Links

One of the best features of a Digital Garden is **interconnectivity**.

## Wikilinks
In Obsidian, you just type `[[` and select a note.
*   Check out the [[formatting]] guide to see how to style things.
*   Go back to the [[index|Homepage]].

## What happens?
When you link notes:
1.  **Backlinks**: At the bottom of the "Formatting" page, you will see a link back here!
2.  **Graph View**: On the right side of the desktop site (or bottom on mobile), you'll see a graph node connecting these two pages.
